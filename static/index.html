                  Notification settings

                  Choose how each kind of alert reaches you on this browser. Critical and volatility pop-ups also show the amber banner at the top of the page.

                  What raises an alert

                  Critical
                  A new story with impact 8 or more, such as an RBI surprise, war escalation or a crude shock.
                  High
                  A new story with impact 7 or more.
                  Move
                  A stock in the news or on your watchlist moves 3% or more today.
                  Volatility
                  India VIX jumps 8% or more.
                  Watchlist
                  Any new story naming a stock on your watchlist.

                  Coverage of the same driver from many outlets alerts once every 90 minutes. Thresholds can be changed in settings.bat.

                  How to read the scores

                  Impact 0 to 10
                  How much the story could move Indian equities. 3 is one small stock, 5 a large cap or a sector, 7 several sectors, 9 or more a market-wide shock.
                  Lean
                  Likely direction for Indian equities, not for the asset in the headline. Rising crude is bearish for India.
                  Sector arrows
                  One arrow is a mild effect, two a strong one. The ± sign means the sector is exposed but direction is unclear.
                  Top bar colours
                  Green or red shows the effect on Indian equities. A rising VIX, dollar or Brent shows in red.
