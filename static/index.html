            Notification settings

            How each kind of alert reaches you in this browser. Critical and volatility pop-ups also show a banner.

            What raises an alert

            Critical
            Market-wide shock: RBI surprise, war escalation, crude spike.
            Market-wide
            Other big macro or geopolitical headlines: Fed, rupee, tariffs, FII flows.
            Stock news
            A tracked stock with a clear catalyst: results, order, deal, regulator, management exit.
            Filing
            An exchange filing (only when NSE or BSE accepts this server).
            Scheduled
            A high-impact data release or expiry is 15 minutes away.
            Volatility
            India VIX up 8% or more on the day.
            Watchlist
            Anything new on a watchlist stock.

            Only items from the last hour alert, and the same driver or stock alerts at most once an hour.

            Indian events

            The calendar covers US, Europe, China and Japan data. Add Indian events (RBI policy, CPI, GDP) to data\events_manual.csv on the server, one per line: 2026-12-04,10:00,RBI policy decision,high. Exchange holidays are not known, so check the expiry date in a holiday week.
