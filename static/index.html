            Notification settings

            Choose how each kind of alert reaches you on this browser. Critical and volatility pop-ups also show the amber banner at the top of the page.

            What raises an alert

            Critical
            Market-wide shock in a headline: RBI surprise, war escalation, crude spike.
            Market-wide
            Other big macro or geopolitical headlines: Fed, rupee, tariffs, FII flows.
            Filing
            A tracked stock files results, an order, M&A, a buyback, a management exit or legal action.
            Stock news
            A headline on a tracked stock with a clear catalyst: results, deal, order, regulator.
            Scheduled
            A high-impact data release or expiry is 15 minutes away.
            Volatility
            India VIX up 8% or more on the day.
            Watchlist
            Anything new on a watchlist stock.

            Only items from the last hour alert, and the same driver or stock alerts at most once an hour. Thresholds are in settings.bat.
